# Caregiver / Activity-Coordinator Interview Guide (15 min)

Purpose: real-world evidence for the Impact criterion. Get consent to quote (first name + role only).
Never record health details or identifying information about the person they care for.

## Opening (1 min)
"We're building a free tool that helps plan reminiscence and music sessions for people with memory
loss. We'd love 15 minutes of your experience. Can we quote you by first name and role?"

## Questions
1. Tell me about how you spend time together / run activities today.
2. When music or old memories come up, what happens? Any moment that stood out?
3. How do you find songs, films, food or topics from their younger years? What's hard?
4. Have you tried playlists, YouTube or AI tools for this? What worked / didn't?
5. (Coordinators) How do you personalise for many residents from different backgrounds? How do you document activities?
6. What topics or songs do you avoid, and why? (Validates the avoid-list feature)
7. If a tool suggested a 20-minute session with prompts, what would make you trust it?
8. (Show the demo, if live) What's confusing? What's missing? Would you use it this week?
9. Who would pay for something like this — family, care home, insurer? Roughly how much?

## After
- Write 3 bullet takeaways + 1–2 verbatim quotes (with consent) in `docs/research/interviews.md`.
- Feed concrete changes into `docs/progress.md` → Decisions.
